## Contact

Email: **unfolio.ltd@gmail.com**

We answer in Hebrew or English.

## Restore purchases

If you reinstalled Unfolio or moved to a new device, you can recover your purchase for free:

1. Open Unfolio and go to **Settings**.
2. Tap **Restore purchases**.

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID that made the purchase. The option is also available on the paywall.

## Manage or cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. Open [apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions](https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions), or on your iPhone go to **Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Unfolio**, and choose what you need.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

## Delete all your data

Everything Unfolio stores — your saved snapshots and hidden accounts — lives only on your device. We have no server and no copy of it.

To erase it: open Unfolio, go to **Settings**, and tap **Delete all local data**. This cannot be undone. Uninstalling the app also removes it.
